Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IFEREWA handles personal information when people use our dating and matchmaking application and related services.

1. Who We Are

IFEREWA is a dating and matchmaking application designed to help adults discover people, communicate, and build meaningful relationships.

IFEREWA is operated by Staircase Nigeria Enterprises. For privacy enquiries, contact lovoradatingsite@gmail.com .

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, IFEREWA may process:

  • Account information such as your email address, username, account identifiers, and authentication data.
  • Profile information such as age or date of birth, gender, height, city, country, relationship goals, interests, biography, and preferences.
  • Profile photographs and other images you choose to upload.
  • Likes, matches, messages, voice notes, and information associated with voice or video calls.
  • Subscription status, plan details, payment references, and payment verification information.
  • Reports, blocks, moderation decisions, and communications with our support team.
  • Technical information such as device details, application diagnostics, network information, and security-related records.
  • Location information that you provide or permit the application to access, where relevant to discovery features.

We do not ask users to publish their passwords or payment card details in their dating profiles.

3. How We Use Information

We use information as necessary to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Display profiles and suggest potential matches.
  • Enable messaging, voice notes, and calling features.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions and verify payments.
  • Send service notifications and account updates.
  • Investigate reports and enforce community guidelines.
  • Prevent fraud, harassment, impersonation, and abuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Profile Visibility

IFEREWA is a social discovery service. Information you include in your visible profile, including photographs, biography, interests, and selected profile attributes, may be displayed to other eligible users.

Do not include sensitive information in your profile that you do not want other users to see.

5. Messages, Voice Notes, and Calls

IFEREWA processes communications to deliver the messaging and calling features. Messages and voice notes may be stored to support conversations and related functions. Call-related technical information may be processed to establish and maintain calls.

Reports of abusive conduct may involve reviewing relevant information in accordance with our safety procedures. We do not represent that communications are end-to-end encrypted unless that protection has been implemented and verified.

6. Photographs, Camera, and Microphone

With your permission, IFEREWA may access your camera, photo library, or microphone to support profile pictures, voice notes, and audio or video calls.

You can manage device permissions through your phone's settings. Disabling permissions may limit certain features.

7. Location Information

IFEREWA may use location information you provide, such as your country, state, or city, to support matching and discovery.

If the application requests device location permission, that information should only be used for the purposes disclosed when permission is requested.

8. Payments and Premium Subscriptions

IFEREWA may offer paid Premium features. Subscription information and payment references may be processed to activate subscriptions, confirm transactions, handle support requests, and prevent fraud.

Payments may be handled by an applicable payment provider. The payment provider's own privacy terms may also apply. Payment methods and providers can differ depending on the platform through which you subscribe.

9. Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that support the operation of IFEREWA, such as hosting, storage, authentication, communications, and payment processing providers, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect users and the security of the service.

Visible profile information is shared with other users as part of the app's matchmaking functionality.

10. Safety and Moderation

IFEREWA provides reporting and blocking features to support user safety. We may process reports and related account information to investigate potential violations of our community rules.

Depending on the circumstances, moderation actions may include warnings, account restrictions, suspension, or account termination.

11. Data Security

We seek to protect personal information through appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and secure communications where implemented.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their login credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and address legitimate operational needs.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods. Some information may need to be retained after account closure where legally required or necessary for fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

13. Account Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request deletion of their IFEREWA account and associated personal data by emailing lovoradatingsite@gmail.com with the subject "IFEREWA Account Deletion Request".

Include the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify that you own the account before processing a request. We will explain any information that must be retained for legal, security, or other legitimate reasons.

Where available, users can also request deletion directly through the application's account settings.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

To make a request, contact lovoradatingsite@gmail.com .

15. Age Restrictions

IFEREWA is intended for adults aged 18 and above. People under 18 are not permitted to create accounts. We may take action to remove accounts found to belong to underage users.

16. International Data Processing

IFEREWA may rely on service providers operating in different countries. As a result, personal information may be processed outside your country of residence. We will seek to apply safeguards required by applicable data protection laws.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy takes effect.
